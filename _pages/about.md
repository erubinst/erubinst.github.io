Hi, I'm Esme, a PhD student in the Robotics Institute at Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) advised by [Prof. Stephen F. Smith](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/ri-faculty/stephen-smith/) working in the [Intelligent Coordination and Logistics Laboratory (ICLL)](http://www.ozone.ri.cmu.edu/). My research focuses on advancing resilient, real-time coordination and scheduling for multi-agent teams. I'm motivated by settings where resources are scarce or constrained and where efficient coordination can meaningfully expand what's possible, from multi-robot teams to care coordination for older adults.
